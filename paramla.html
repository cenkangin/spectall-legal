paramla is moving to SpectAll

SpectAll (SpA) is paramla's next global version. paramla retires on December 31, 2026. Move your portfolios to SpA together with their histories in just a few minutes before then.

What's new in SpectAll?

• 180K+ instruments on 60+ exchanges worldwide - from New York to Shanghai, London to Johannesburg, Frankfurt to Toronto
• Brand new categories from 'Fixed Income' to 'Derivative', 'Private Market' to 'Real Estate', 'Vehicle' and 'Collectible'
• Daily Change per 'your day', wherever you are around the world
• Multi-currency costing in certain categories
• Up to 50 portfolios
• Picking portfolio when recording
• Holding transfer between portfolios
• Personal sorting of portfolios
• Notes to your holdings
• Reports
• 135 currencies
• 3 themes: Light | Default | Dark

Price

Free for 30 days, then ₺49.99 a month

What you need to do

1. Install SpectAll from App Store or Google Play
2. In SpectAll, open Settings > 'Move from paramla' and get your code
3. In paramla, open Settings > Device Management > Add Device > Source Device, enter your code from SpectAll and confirm
4. Once your data appears in SpectAll, repeat steps 1 to 3 on your other devices - if any - to pair them too
5. Cancel your paramla subscription in your App Store or Google Play account; deleting paramla does not end your subscription

What moves?

• All your portfolios, together with their histories from January 2, 2025: each holding starts with the quantity and average cost you held as of that day and every change after it follows exactly as you recorded it; your profit/loss stays the same (only for those holdings you sold and later bought again, SpectAll's standard 'Moving Average Cost' method may result in differences)
• Your favourites (as pinned assets), language and currency preferences

What does not move?

Your paramla subscription: cancel it in your store account (step 5 above); like paramla, SpectAll is also fully anonymous asking for no name, e-mail or password

Assets SpectAll does not carry

In case SpectAll does not carry a few of your assets (delisted, closed, or some coins), they move to 'Other' category at their value on the day you move with a note saying what they were; their value no longer follows the market and you can update or delete them any time
